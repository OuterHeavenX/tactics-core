How to play
1. Click a blue unit to select it.
2. Click a highlighted tile to move.
3. Click a red enemy in range to attack.
4. Press W or the Wait button to end its turn.
5. Priest: click a blue ally in range to cast Cure (8 MP).

Turn order is Charge-Time based, like FFT: faster units act more often. Defeat all enemies to win!
